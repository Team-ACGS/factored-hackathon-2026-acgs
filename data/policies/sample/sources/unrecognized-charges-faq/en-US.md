## Before you open a claim

### How do I know whether a charge is mine?

Check the merchant name, the amount and the date in the transaction details.
Many companies charge under a name that is different from the brand you know, and an online purchase may show the name of the payment platform.
If you are unsure, Clara can review the transaction with you in {{policy.channels.app_clara}}.

### What happens if the charge is still pending?

A pending charge younger than {{policy.holds.fresh_hold}} can still change or disappear, because the merchant has not confirmed it yet.
It is best to wait until it is confirmed before you open a claim, unless you do not recognize the merchant at all.

### How long do I have to report a charge?

You can report it up to {{policy.claims.report_window}} after the date of the statement it appears on.
The sooner you report it, the easier it is for the bank to gather the records from the merchant and the card network.

### Does a charge made by a family member count as unrecognized?

If someone in your family used the card with your permission, the charge is not unauthorized use and a claim does not apply.
Talk to that person first; if the charge still has no explanation, Clara helps you review it.

## How to open a claim

### Where do I open a claim?

In the app, with Clara, who asks for the details of the charge and opens the case for you.
You can also call {{policy.channels.phone}}, which answers {{policy.channels.phone_schedule}}.

### Can I open a claim while I am traveling?

Yes. You can use the app from anywhere or call {{policy.channels.phone_abroad}}.
If you also lost the card, Clara helps you confirm the block in the same conversation, and you are always the one who confirms the block.

### Can I talk to a person?

Yes. Clara hands you over to a person on the service team when you ask, with a summary of what you have already discussed.
People answer the chat {{policy.service.handoff_hours}}.
Outside those hours, Clara leaves your case ready so a person can pick it up as soon as service opens.

### What information do I need?

Only the transaction you do not recognize and a short description of what happened.
If you have proof, such as an email from the merchant or a screenshot, you can attach it later in the app.

## During the review

### How long does the review take?

The review of your claim takes up to {{policy.claims.review_time}} after your case is {{policy.claims.stage_assigned}} to an analyst.
The bank lets you know in the app at every change of stage.

### Will the bank ask me for documents?

Sometimes the analyst needs evidence, such as the receipt of a refund from the merchant.
You have {{policy.claims.evidence_window}} to send it in the app or to {{policy.channels.email}}.

### Where do I follow my case?

In {{policy.channels.app_claims}}, where you see the stage of each claim, the analyst's messages and the estimated date of the answer.

### Do I get my money back during the review?

Where the policy allows it, the bank may apply a provisional credit within {{policy.claims.provisional_credit_time}}.
That credit is not the final decision and may be reversed if the review confirms that the charge is correct.

## After the decision

### How do I learn about the decision?

When your case is {{policy.claims.stage_resolved}}, the bank sends you the decision and its reasons in the app.
If the decision is in your favor, the adjustment shows on your statement.
If the decision confirms the charge, the bank explains which records it reviewed and why it concluded that the purchase was valid.

### Can the bank close my case without telling me?

No. The bank lets you know before it closes your case, and your case is only {{policy.claims.stage_closed}} after you receive the decision.
While the case is open, you can write to Clara to find out where it stands.

### What can I do if I disagree?

You can ask the bank to reopen your case up to {{policy.claims.reopen_window}} after it closes, with new information.
If you are still not satisfied, you can write to the bank's ombudsman office ({{policy.service.ombudsman_name}}) at {{policy.service.ombudsman}}.

### Will I get a new card?

If the charge came from unauthorized use, the bank offers to block the card and send you a new one, which arrives in {{policy.cards.replacement_time}}.
You are always the one who confirms the block, and the new card is free of charge in that case.
